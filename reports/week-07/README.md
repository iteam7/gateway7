# Week 07 report — Gateway7

## Scope and findings

This report indexes the Week 7 work in progress: the team is designing the `UT-01` usability test and preparing its private data, and no usability result is claimed.
The design, the instrumentation, the Sprint Review and `REL-07` follow; the results are due with Submission 8 on 21 November 2026.

## Usability test

The `UT-01` design is being completed under [#267](https://github.com/iteam7/gateway7/issues/267).
It carries the goal, the research questions, the participant profile, the recruitment target, the tasks, the surveys, the protocol, the instrumentation, the analysis plan and the evidence map.

The private usability-data repository, the consent template and the recordings folder are prepared under [#269](https://github.com/iteam7/gateway7/issues/269).
The test's instrumentation is implemented under [#268](https://github.com/iteam7/gateway7/issues/268): the product metric recipes exclude `source=usability-test`, and a session is read through its own staging filter and time window.

No counted session has run, no participant data is committed, and the Week 7 report claims no usability result.

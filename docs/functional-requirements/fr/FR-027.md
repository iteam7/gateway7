# FR-027: Audit Versioned Administrative Changes

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [Repository owner task #295](https://github.com/iteam7/gateway7/issues/295), [FR-021](FR-021.md), [FR-023](FR-023.md), [ADR-010](../../architecture/adr/ADR-010.md)
- **Containers:** `gateway`

## Behavior

Every committed administrative change shall record an identified actor, action, target, timestamp, request identifier and safe change summary in protected durable audit history.
The state change and its audit entry shall commit atomically.
Raw credentials, digests, encrypted secret values and conversation content shall not enter audit projections.
Each administrative write shall require the current management revision; a stale revision shall produce a conflict and preserve later state.
The console shall refresh after a conflict instead of resubmitting an obsolete full profile automatically.
Audit inspection shall support actor, action, target, UTC period and bounded pagination.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given two operators or tabs holding the same revision, when one narrows a client's permissions and the other attempts an outdated enable, suspension or profile edit, then the second receives a conflict and cannot restore the earlier permissions.
The successful change has one matching audit entry after restart.

### AC-02

Given credential, pricing and policy changes followed by an injected persistence failure, when protected audit history is inspected, then only committed changes appear, each names its actor and target, the failed change leaves no partial state or audit row, and neither generated tokens nor provider secrets appear.

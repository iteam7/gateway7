# QR-06: Learn The Gateway On A First Request

A developer new to Gateway7 sends a first request on staging and rates the task's ease at a median of at least five of seven across the counted usability sessions.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Learnability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

A backend developer who has not used Gateway7 before, evaluating it for a small team.

### Stimulus

The first chat request through the staging gateway, sent with a client token, whose answer shows the company policy applied to the message.

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university VM, reached from the university network, with the synthetic provider and the seed reset before the session.

### Artifact

The gateway's HTTP API and the session's task materials, as described in [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md).

### Response

The participant reaches the main task's end state and rates the task's ease on the standard SEQ after the task.

### Response Measure

Across the counted sessions, the median SEQ rating for the main task is at least five of seven.
The SEQ is the standard question, `Overall, how difficult or easy was the task to complete?`, answered from 1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy).
The statistic is the median, which [UT-01's analysis plan](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#analysis-plan) reports.

## QRT-01

The main task of `UT-01` reaches a median SEQ rating of at least five of seven across at least three counted sessions.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** [`UT-01`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) in the staging deployment on the university VM, from a machine on the university network, reading [its results](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md).

### Why Manual

No command can judge a participant's rating of a task.
The measure is a human judgement recorded by the SEQ instrument after the main task, and it reads no `SLI`.

### Procedure

1. Recruit at least three representative participants who match [the participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile), and collect the durable [consent](../../usability-tests/ut-01/consent.md) before each counted session.
2. Run the main task exactly as [the record](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#the-tasks) defines it, on staging, from the reset synthetic seed, in person on the university network.
3. Ask the standard SEQ question after the main task, record the rating, and keep the transcript and the raw notes in the private usability-data repository.
4. Put the main task's SEQ ratings in [results.md](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), compute the median across the counted sessions, and report the sample size.
5. Record the result, the sprint, and the sanitized evidence in the [testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan).

**Pass when:** across at least three counted sessions, the median SEQ rating for the main task is at least five of seven.

The bar is fixed before the first counted session, and the team does not coach a participant toward it or change it after the data.
The session's other measures stay analysis results and do not change this verdict.
